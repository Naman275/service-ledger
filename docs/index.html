Service Ledger

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026
Developer: Naman Singla
Support and privacy contact: rudratheternity@gmail.com

In short: Service Ledger keeps your customer, service and payment records on your device. It has no account, developer-operated backend, advertising or analytics. Information leaves the app only when you choose to share it or export a backup.

1. Scope

This policy describes the Service Ledger Android app provided by Naman Singla (Google Play application ID: com.namansingla.serviceledger). It also covers the pilot debug app, dev.serviceledger.app.debug.

2. Information you enter and why it is used

The app stores customer names, optional phone numbers and addresses; optional appliance details; service descriptions, parts, charges and service dates; payment amounts, dates, methods, references and notes; correction reasons; payment due dates and service follow-up dates; and optional appliance or service photos.

This information is used to maintain your ledger, calculate balances, display service and payment history, and prepare the reminders or summaries you request. Payment entries are bookkeeping records: the app does not process payments, access bank accounts or issue refunds. Do not enter card security codes, bank passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

If you record another person's information, you are responsible for having an appropriate basis to do so, informing them where required, and entering only what you need for your services.

3. On-device storage and developer access

Ledger records and copied photos remain in app-private storage on your Android device. The app does not automatically upload them to Naman Singla or to a developer-operated server. It has no login, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or location access, and does not request internet permission. No ledger data is sold by the developer.

The database is not independently password-encrypted. App-private storage and Android device protection are not guarantees against someone with access to an unlocked or compromised phone. Use a strong screen lock, keep Android updated and protect your backups.

4. Contacts and photos

Contact import uses Android's picker to read the name and number of the one phone entry you select. The app does not request permission to read your entire address book. You can review and edit the selected information before saving.

Photo selection uses Android's system photo or document picker, not broad photo-library access. The selected image is copied into private storage, oriented, resized and compressed. The saved copy omits original EXIF metadata, including location tags. The app does not scan your gallery or access your camera. Removing an attachment does not delete its gallery original.

5. Sharing and WhatsApp reminders

When you choose to share a service summary or reminder, its text is passed to the destination app you choose. Sharing currently sends text only, not attached photos.

When you open a payment reminder in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, the recipient number and prepared reminder text are passed to that app. You can review the number and message before opening WhatsApp, and must press Send there yourself. Service Ledger does not automatically message customers or receive delivery or read status.

WhatsApp and other destination apps handle the information under their own privacy policies. Copies you send to other people or apps are not removed when you delete local ledger records.

6. Backups

You can explicitly export a password-encrypted backup using Android's file picker. Backups include ledger records, payment and correction history, and referenced photos. If you select a cloud document provider, that provider handles the exported file under its own terms. There is no automatic cloud synchronization or Android automatic app backup.

Backup passwords are not saved by the app and cannot be recovered by the developer. Keep the password and an off-device backup safe. Restoring a backup replaces the current records rather than merging them. Previously exported copies must be managed separately.

7. Notifications

Optional Android notifications remind you that service follow-ups are due. The notification shows a follow-up count, not customer names, phone numbers or service notes. You control notification permission in Android settings. Notifications do not send messages to customers.

8. Retention, correction and deletion

Records remain on the device until you delete eligible records, replace the ledger by restoring a backup, clear app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. The app does not impose an automatic expiry period.

You can edit customer and service information. Incorrect payments can be voided with a reason: the amount stops counting as received, but the original entry and correction remain visible. Ordinary deletion of customers or services with payment history is blocked to protect bookkeeping history. This does not prevent you from clearing all app storage or uninstalling the app to remove all local records.

Deleting an eligible customer removes their appliances and services. Deleting an appliance preserves its services and payments under the customer. After successful record changes or restore, unreferenced private photos are cleaned up. A photo chosen in a canceled draft may remain until a later cleanup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes these copies.

Clearing storage or uninstalling cannot remove backups you exported or information already shared with other apps or people. Delete those copies separately where appropriate.

9. Privacy requests and support

For questions, contact rudratheternity@gmail.com. The developer cannot remotely inspect, correct or delete your local ledger. If your details were recorded by a service provider, contact that provider to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

If you voluntarily email support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email provider under its own terms. Do not send customer records, backup passwords or payment credentials in support messages. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations.

10. Children

Service Ledger is intended for adults managing service-business records. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly solicit children's personal information. Contact the privacy address if you believe such information has been sent to the developer.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Material changes, such as adding account-based cloud synchronization, will be disclosed in the app or accompanying release information as appropriate.
