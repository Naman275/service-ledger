Service Ledger stores customer names, optional phone numbers and addresses,
appliance details, repair notes, parts, charges, cumulative amounts received,
service dates and optional appliance/service photos entered by the app user.
Technicians should have an appropriate basis
to record their customers' details and should only enter necessary information.

In this version, these records remain in the app's private storage on the
device. The app does not operate a server or automatically upload records.
There is no account, advertising, analytics SDK or location access.

If you choose to import a phone contact, Android lets you select one phone
entry. The app reads that entry's name and phone number, not the full address
book. You can review/edit these values before saving them as a customer.
The app does not request full Contacts permission.

If you choose a photo through Android's system picker, the app copies a
compressed version into its private storage. It applies orientation and
removes original photo metadata, including EXIF location tags. It does not
scan your photo library or request broad media-storage permission.

You may explicitly share a reminder or service summary with another app.
That sends the selected text to the destination you choose; the destination's
privacy practices then apply.
Current sharing sends text only, not attached photos.

You may explicitly export a password-encrypted backup through Android's file
picker. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the
backup file under its own terms. Backups include referenced photos and payment
amounts as well as the text records. The app cannot recover a forgotten password.
No automatic cloud backup or multi-device sync is provided.

Optional notifications remind you of due services. The lock-screen notification
contains an appliance count, not customer names, phone numbers or repair notes.
Android settings control whether notifications are allowed.

You can edit or delete records inside the app. Deleting a customer also deletes
their appliances and service history; deleting an appliance deletes its visits.
After successful record changes or restore, private photos not referenced by
any saved record are removed. A photo selected in a canceled draft can remain
until that cleanup. Removing an attachment does not delete its gallery original.
Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local records. Files you
previously exported and information shared to other apps are not removed by
these actions; manage those copies separately.

Device-private storage is not a guarantee against access to an unlocked or
compromised phone. Use a screen lock, keep the device updated, and keep backups
and their passwords secure.
