APP NAME
Service Ledger

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Track customers, services and payments offline, with WhatsApp payment reminders

FULL DESCRIPTION
Keep your service work and payment records together in one simple, offline ledger.
Service Ledger helps independent service providers record customers, track work
and see what is still to collect. No account or sign-in is needed.

CUSTOMERS AND SERVICES
Add customers and record services directly against them. Link an appliance when
it is useful, add an optional photo, and keep service charges, dates, parts and
follow-ups with the job.

A CLEAR PAYMENT HISTORY
Record individual payments against a service, including partial payments.
Choose cash, UPI, bank transfer, card or another method. See total charges,
receipts and outstanding balances across your ledger and for each customer.
Correct an incorrect payment by voiding it with a reason while keeping its
history visible.

PAYMENT REMINDERS YOU CONTROL
Find pending and overdue payments, preview a reminder, then open WhatsApp or
WhatsApp Business to send it yourself. WhatsApp must be installed, and recipient
numbers must include an international country code. Messages are not sent
automatically, and the app does not track delivery.

YOUR RECORDS STAY ON YOUR DEVICE
Use the ledger without an internet connection. There are no ads or analytics.
Export a password-encrypted backup and restore it when needed. Keep an
off-device backup and its password safe: there is no automatic cloud sync or
password recovery. Restoring replaces the destination ledger.

DESIGNED FOR EVERYDAY WORK
Distinct service, balance and receipt cards help separate the information.
Light and dark themes, customer search, payment history and optional service
follow-up notifications keep your records easy to find.

Amounts are recorded in Indian rupees (INR). Service Ledger records payments
for bookkeeping; it does not collect money, connect to bank accounts or process
UPI/card transactions. WhatsApp is a separate service and needs connectivity
to send messages. Service Ledger is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
